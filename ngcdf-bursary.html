Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to apply for NG-CDF bursary?
Any student currently enrolled in a secondary school, TVET college, or university can apply. Simply fill out the application form and select your preferred bursary amount.
How long does the application process take?
Applications are reviewed within 2-4 weeks of submission. You will receive a notification once your application has been processed.
How are the bursary funds disbursed?
Approved funds are paid directly to the student's institution and credited to their fee account. Students do not receive cash.
Can I apply more than once per year?
Each student may apply once per financial year. Multiple applications from the same student will be flagged as duplicates.
How do I choose which bursary amount to apply for?
Select the amount that best reflects your financial need. Higher amounts are prioritised for orphans, students with disabilities, and those from extremely vulnerable households.
